# 2. Character Manual

This file tracks **Who You Are**.

## **Primary Resources**

These are the numbers you track in your App.

### **1. Level & XP**

- **Level Cap:** 1000
- **XP:** Gained from Missions. It never resets; it accumulates.
- **Next Level Cost:** `(Current Level ^ 2) * 10`
  - _Level 1:_ 10 XP
  - _Level 10:_ 1,000 XP
  - _Level 50:_ 25,000 XP

### **2. Level Points (LP)**

- **Purpose:** The currency used to upgrade Skill/Magic Ranks. You can also learn a whole new skill for 5-11 LP (`5 + 1d6`). After choosing a skill to learn you will run the dice and it will determine the price and then you will write it in the description of the skill/magic.
- **Gain:** Every time you Level Up, you gain `1d10 + 5` LP.

### **3. Memory Slots (Loadout)**

- **Purpose:** The number of Skills or Magic spells you can have _active_ at once. You can "know" infinite skills, but only "equip" a few.
- **Limit:** `3 + (Level / 10)`
  - _Ex:_ Level 20 = 5 Slots.

### **4. Money**

- **Purpose:** Buy Items/Gear.
- **Gain:** Mission Rewards.

### **5. Ki (Energy)**

- **Purpose:** Fuel for Magic.
- **Max Ki:** `((Level - 1) ^ 2) * 10`
- **Regen:** Full restore after a Long Rest (End of Session/Mission).

---

## **Abilities: Skills vs. Magic**

### **Skills (Physical/Tech)**

- **Cost:** **0 Ki**. Infinite use.
- **Theme:** Human potential pushed to the limit (Sniping, Parkour, Hacking, Martial Arts).
- **Power Source:** Training and Equipment.

### **Magic (Supernatural)**

- **Cost:** **Consumes Ki** (Amount defined in description).
- **Theme:** Reality bending (Fireball, Teleport, Summoning).
- **Power Source:** Mana and Ki.

---

## **Items**

- **Purchasable:** Bought with Money. Have a "Max Quantity" (e.g., 5 Potions).
- **Non-Purchasable:** Relics/Artifacts found as Loot. Unique and powerful.

> [!TIP] > **Power Cap Rule:** No single Item, Skill, or Magic can ever contribute more than **500 Power** to your roll.
