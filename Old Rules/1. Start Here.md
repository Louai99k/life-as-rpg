# 1. Start Here

## Welcome to **Life as RPG**

**Genre:** Solo Urban Fantasy / LitRPG  
**Concept:** The modern world is changing. You have "Awakened" and now see the world through a Game System.

### **The Goal**

Your objective is simple: **Grow Stronger.**
You will complete missions, earn rewards, and upgrade your abilities to survive the increasing supernatural threats hiding in everyday life.

### **How to Play**

This game is played in a loop tailored to be manageable, not overwhelming.

**1. Prepare (The Setup)**

- Check your **[[2. Character Manual|Character Manual]]** to see your current stats.
- Decide on a move or roll for a Mission.

**2. The Mission (The Action)**

- Use **[[4. Missions and Rewards|Mission & Rewards]]** to generate an objective.
- Use **[[3. Core Rules|Core Rules]]** to resolve conflicts (Fight, Hack, Negotiate).
- Use **[[5. Oracle Reference|Oracle Reference]]** to answer questions (Yes/No, Locations, Twists).

**3. Reward (The Growth)**

- Gain XP and Loot.
- Upgrade your Skills or Magic.
- Rest and repeat.

### **Files Guide**

- **[[2. Character Manual]]:** Everything about _You_ (Level, Ki, Money).
- **[[3. Core Rules]]:** How to roll dice and win fights.
- **[[4. Missions and Rewards]]:** How to generate content and get paid.
- **[[5. Oracle Reference]]:** The tables you need to play (keep this open!).
